Why Purple Iris?

Because the little things
deserve a little love.

There’s something special about a bar of soap made by hand, a candle that softens the room, or a kit that gets everyone making something together. That’s the feeling behind Purple Iris Crafts, Soaps and More.

We make goat milk soaps, candles with essential oils and botanical touches, and plaster art kits — each one a chance to bring a little more care, calm or creativity into your day.
